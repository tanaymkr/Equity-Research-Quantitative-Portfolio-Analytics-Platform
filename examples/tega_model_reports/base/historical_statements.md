## Notes

- This is a historical FY2025 case, not a current Tega valuation. Later acquisitions, financing and FY2026 results are not included.
- All currency amounts and share counts use million units. One crore equals ten million.
- Rounded statement dashes are represented as zero only where the source reports a dash. Noncontrolling interests are below the report's rounding threshold, not proven economically zero.
- Assets, liabilities and equity preserve reported line items, with term/current debt and payables aggregated into analytical pools. Debt pools exclude accrued interest, which remains in other financial liabilities.
- Historical debt reconciliations include accrued interest as explicitly named; their closing amounts are not identical to principal-only balance-sheet pools.
- Historic rounding differences up to INR 0.02 million are disclosed and checked; no balancing entry is added.
- Cash capex is the investing cash outflow for capital assets. Asset additions, CWIP transfers and noncash lease additions have different scopes and must not be substituted for cash capex.
