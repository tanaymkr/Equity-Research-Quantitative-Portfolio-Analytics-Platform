## Assumptions and limitations

- Legacy-only counterfactual from 31 March 2026. It excludes the subsequent Molycop acquisition, parent acquisition loan and contribution. Fundraising cash therefore remains in this control model. Do not use its equity value as a current Tega target or add it to the acquisition DCF.
- The default run_tega_model.py remains the acquisition valuation and reads the same complete FY26 statement source. This legacy-only model demonstrates a fully linked annual income statement, balance sheet and cash flow from the audited FY26 close.
- Growth, weighted operating margins, working-capital ratios and cash capex are initially aligned to the first five years of the acquisition base case, with full FY2027 periods here. They are editable analyst assumptions, not automatic links to that separate assumptions file.
- FY2027 term and lease principal repayments start at the disclosed March 2026 current maturities. Later payments and all coupons are assumptions; no contractual amortization ladder is claimed.
- Opening asset groups use FY26 reported net values. Remaining lives are analyst assumptions. Land is not depreciated; goodwill is not amortized. New commissioned assets receive half-year charges.
- Cash capex enters CWIP/development. Equal new lease asset/liability additions are noncash financing and economic investment for FCFF. Historical source differences between asset and lease additions remain visible.
- Receivables, inventories and payables drive working capital. Other operating balances, existing deferred taxes, other bank balances and investments are held constant; their future income and realization are not forecast.
- The assumed debt rates, facility limit and cash sweeps are modeling conventions, not verified commitments. Other income, future OCI, FX, acquisitions, equity issuance and tax-loss carryforwards are not forecast. Tax expense equals cash tax on positive modeled taxable income.
- JV income is after JV tax; no additional group-level tax is assumed. No JV distribution is assumed, consistent with FY26 cash receipts of zero.
- The fiscal-year-end reference is 31 March 2026 using the annual report published 28 August 2026. This is not a point-in-time backtest. Terminal growth, WACC and ROIC are analyst assumptions.

The JSON output contains the complete inputs, source locators and unrounded calculated amounts.
