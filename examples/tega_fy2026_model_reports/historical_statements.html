Notes

  • FY2025 and FY2026 consolidated actuals from the FY2025-26 annual report. FY2025 comparatives agree with the earlier reported-history file. These accounts close before the Molycop acquisition on 1 June 2026.
  • All monetary values are INR million; shares_outstanding_million uses million shares. EPS is INR/share. Other reserves exclude retained earnings. Statement dashes and NCI below the reporting rounding threshold are represented by zero.
  • Term debt includes current maturities; the short-term pool excludes those maturities. Interest accrued remains in other financial liabilities. Note 41(c) debt roll-forwards include accrued interest and are reconciled to these principal pools.
  • Reported operating EBITDA is revenue minus materials, inventory-change expense, employee expense and other expense. It excludes other income and includes the INR775.77m acquisition transaction expense. Adjusted EBITDA is a separate modeling choice.
  • FY2026 other bank balances include INR9585.33m deposits and INR0.48m unpaid dividend accounts. INR69.46m of the deposits are pledged. The cash/debt bridge excludes pledged and unpaid-dividend balances from available liquidity.
  • Primary cash-flow lines retain the report's signs and groupings. Cash capex INR1359.36m is distinct from asset/CWIP additions. ROU asset additions INR337.79m differ from new lease liabilities INR337.72m; both reported amounts are preserved.
  • PPE, ROU, intangible, CWIP, debt, equity and cash reconciliations retain source rounding. No balancing plug is inserted. FY2025 cash-flow finance addback differs from P&L finance cost as reported.
  • The standalone linked forecast is a legacy-only counterfactual from 31 March 2026; the default acquisition DCF separately applies acquisition funding and ownership. Do not add the two equity valuations together.
  • The complete acquisition-era consolidated balance sheet still needs detailed post-close balances and purchase-accounting inputs; these March statements do not establish those June values.
